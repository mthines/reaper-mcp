What happened: Building "upstream density" for a loud+clean metal master, I added a
Free Clip transient clipper to the DRUMS bus driven +3 dB in / −3 dB out
(net-neutral level). RMS was unchanged, but the user said "I can't hear the drums
anymore." Pairing it with taming a saturator (JST Heat dropped from aggressive
"Drum Power" down to subtle) compounded the presence loss.
Why: Clipping ~3 dB off a FULL drum bus shaves the kick/snare ATTACK transients —
exactly the peaks that let the kit cut through a dense guitar wall. So the kit
recedes even at the same RMS. Net-neutral RMS ≠ preserved perceived punch.
Do next time: On a full drum bus, keep clipping GENTLE (≈1–1.5 dB), or clip the
kick/snare INDIVIDUALLY where shaving attack is less destructive — preserve the
transient attack. Don't simultaneously back off drum saturation/presence while
clipping; that stacks two presence losses. Fix that worked here: ease the drum
clipper to ≈+1/−1 and restore some saturator presence. (context: loud dense metal,
full drum bus, "make it cut" goal)
