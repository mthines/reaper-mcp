An assistant that forgets everything between sessions is a lot of re-explaining. That's what [LoreKit](https://lorekit.io) fixes: a free, simple memory the assistant reads from and writes to.

## What it remembers

- **Plugins.** How a plugin behaves and the settings that tend to work, from [`/learn-plugin`](teach-it-a-plugin.md) or from the first time it meets one.
- **Lessons.** Things that went wrong, or that you corrected. "This singer's sibilance peaks around 7 kHz." "I never want the master limiter above -1 dB."

Two layers: a built-in one (shared knowledge about common plugins, read-only) and your own, which wins when they disagree.

## What it doesn't do

It isn't watching your audio or your files. It stores short notes written in words.

## Seeing and tidying it

> say: /mix-memory status

Other options: `recall` (what's relevant now), `consolidate` (merge duplicates). Anything it wrote is under your account and you can delete it.

## Do I need it?

No, but it's the difference between "gets to know you" and "starts fresh every time". Setup is quick, and the [install guide](install.md) lists it as recommended.
